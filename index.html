Method

Decision

  • Noon on D-1, after the ERCOT day-ahead market clears. Telemetry and RT prices known through HE11.
  • Battery DP plans 24 h of charge/discharge on the price forecast (300 MW / 1,200 MWh, 87% round trip).
  • Commitment DP picks turbine blocks online per hour: start and no-load costs, scenario-expected dispatch cost.

Uncertainty

  • Scenarios: the model's own joint (price, load) errors from the previous 21 settled days, same hour.
  • Reliability floor: blocks + 400 MW import must cover the upper load bound at the chosen level.
  • Settlement cancels charging, then discharges the battery, before counting unserved load.

Gate

  • Regret = settled cost minus the same process with perfect foresight.
  • Daily regret differences, 7-day moving-block bootstrap (2,000 resamples), 90% interval.
  • BLOCK on any reliability regression or a significant cost increase; PROMOTE only if significantly cheaper.
